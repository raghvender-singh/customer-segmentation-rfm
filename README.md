# Customer Segmentation & RFM Analysis

## Business Problem

The marketing team wants to understand customer behavior and identify which customer segments should receive priority in retention campaigns.

The analysis aims to answer:

**Which customer segments should receive priority in retention campaigns, and how should marketing resources be allocated based on customer value?**

---

## Project Objective

- Analyze customer purchasing behavior using RFM analysis.
- Segment customers based on Recency, Frequency, and Monetary value.
- Identify high-value and at-risk customer segments.
- Provide actionable recommendations for customer retention.
- Build an interactive Tableau dashboard to communicate key insights.

---

## Dataset

The analysis uses the **Online Retail II** dataset, which contains transactional data from an online retail business.

The dataset includes information such as:

- Invoice number
- Stock code
- Product description
- Quantity
- Invoice date
- Unit price
- Customer ID
- Country

## Data Cleaning & Preparation

The raw dataset was cleaned and prepared before performing the RFM analysis.

Key steps included:

- Removed records with missing Customer IDs.
- Removed duplicate records.
- Converted InvoiceDate into datetime format.
- Removed transactions with non-positive Quantity.
- Removed transactions with non-positive Price.
- Created a Revenue column using Quantity × Price.

---

## RFM Analysis

RFM analysis was used to understand customer purchasing behavior.

### Recency

Measures how recently a customer made a purchase.

### Frequency

Measures how frequently a customer made purchases, based on unique invoices.

### Monetary

Measures the total historical revenue generated by a customer.

Each customer was assigned RFM scores from 1 to 5, where higher scores generally represent more valuable or recent customer behavior.

---

## Customer Segmentation

Customers were grouped into six segments based on their RFM scores:

| Segment | Description |
|---|---|
| Champions | Recent, frequent, and high-value customers |
| Loyal Customers | Frequent customers with strong customer value |
| Potential Loyalists | Recent customers with potential to become more frequent buyers |
| At Risk | Historically valuable customers who have become inactive |
| Hibernating | Inactive customers with low historical value |
| Others | Customers who do not fit the defined segment criteria |

---

## Key Insights

- **Champions** represent approximately 22% of customers but contribute **68.13% of historical revenue**.
- **At Risk** customers represent **10.43% of customers** and contribute **8.65% of historical revenue**.
- **Potential Loyalists** represent **11.62% of customers** and show relatively recent purchasing behavior but lower purchase frequency.
- **Hibernating** customers represent approximately 22% of customers but contribute only **1.86% of historical revenue**.

---

## Business Recommendations

### 1. Champions — Retention Priority

Prioritize retention of high-value customers through personalized offers, loyalty benefits, and early access to products.

### 2. At Risk — Win-back Priority

Target historically valuable but inactive customers with personalized reactivation campaigns.

### 3. Potential Loyalists — Growth Opportunity

Encourage repeat purchases through product recommendations, bundles, and loyalty incentives.

### 4. Hibernating — Low-Cost Engagement

Use lower-cost reactivation campaigns rather than allocating a large share of the retention budget to this segment.

## Limitations
The dataset does not contain marketing campaign costs.
Campaign conversion or response data is not available.
Incremental revenue from retention campaigns cannot be measured.
Therefore, actual campaign ROI cannot be calculated from this dataset.
The recommendations represent RFM-based retention priorities, rather than measured campaign ROI.

## Conclusion

The analysis demonstrates how RFM-based customer segmentation can help businesses understand customer value and prioritize retention efforts.

The findings show a strong concentration of historical revenue among Champions, while At Risk customers represent an opportunity for targeted win-back efforts.

The Tableau dashboard provides an interactive view of customer segments and supports data-driven retention planning.
